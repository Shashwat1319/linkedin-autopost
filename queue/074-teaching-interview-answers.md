Teaching improved my interview answers. Here's the exact change:

Before: "I implemented JWT authentication using jsonwebtoken and bcrypt."

After: "Students kept asking why we can't just store the login in localStorage.
So I taught the tradeoffs — and my app now uses short-lived access tokens
in memory with an httpOnly refresh cookie. Fewer words. Real reasoning."

Interviewers don't want TERMS.
They want: situation → decision → tradeoff → result.

Teaching forced me to find the "why" behind everything I claimed to know.
Then interviews stopped feeling like memorization.
