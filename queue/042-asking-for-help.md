How to ask for help without looking clueless:

❌ "It doesn't work. Please help."
❌ Screenshot of terminal with 400 lines, no context
✅ "X doesn't work. Expected Y, got Z. Here's my minimal repro. Here's what I already tried."

The4-part formula:
1. What I'm trying to do
2. What I expected
3. What actually happened
4. What I've already ruled out

Why it matters: seniors aren't annoyed by questions. They're annoyed by questions that made them reconstruct your entire context.

Respect the reader's time → get better answers → look more senior than you are.
