Story from my internship that still makes me smile:

4 PM. Demo at 4:30.
Frontend deployed. Backend deployed.
Browser says: blocked by CORS.

Not a syntax error. Not logic. A missing header on ONE endpoint.

Panic → breathe → read the actual error → fix in 4 minutes → demo at 4:31.

What I carried out of that day:
→ Read the error text. All of it. Before touching code.
→ Keep a personal "prod issues" list — patterns repeat
→ CORS belongs in your deploy checklist, not your crisis kit

Experience is just a list of bugs you've already paid for.

Which error message has wasted the most hours of your life?
