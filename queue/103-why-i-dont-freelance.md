Option A: freelancing (client work, revisions, invoices, uncertainty)
→ Money NOW, but steals hours from the ONE thing I want: a full-time dev role by Dec 31

Option B: job hunt + skills + visibility
→ Slower cash, but compounds into salary + growth

The math that decided it:
→ Freelance: ₹5-10k/month, variable hours
→ Junior role: ₹25-35k + learning + trajectory

Later, freelancing becomes optional (choose clients, not bills).

Strategic saying NO is a career skill. Every yes is a no to something else.
