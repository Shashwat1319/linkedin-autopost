The client email that saved a project (and my evening):

OLD instinct: "Sure, I can add that. Maybe tonight."

Better reply:
"Yes, this is doable. It touches auth + the dashboard — I estimate2 days.
Want me to slot it AFTER the current deliverable, or swap priorities?"

Three things that message does:
1. Prices the ask (they learn scope = time)
2. Offers a choice (you're a partner, not a vending machine)
3. Protects the deadline everyone already agreed on

Every "quick change" is quick for THEM. Your evening is not.

Freelance or employed: scope creep dies the moment time has a number attached.
