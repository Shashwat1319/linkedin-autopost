I rebuilt my portfolio from scratch. Then I audited 10 others. Same mistakes:

1. "Passionate developer" hero with zero evidence
2. Skills listed as percentages (React: 92%? means nothing)
3. Projects without LIVE links — "code on GitHub" buried in text
4. Contact form that goes nowhere (check your EmailJS, seriously)
5. No resume link in the first screen
6. Loads in 4 seconds — you lost them at 3

My rebuild checklist:
→ One sentence: who you are + what you want
→ 3 projects with live demo + code + YOUR specific role
→ Resume downloadable in one click
→ Mobile-first, tested on a real phone

Your portfolio isn't art. It's a conversion funnel for interviews.

Which of those 6 mistakes does YOUR site have right now?
