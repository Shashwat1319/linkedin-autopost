How I answer "Tell me about yourself" (and it works):

Structure: Present → Past → Future. 60 seconds max.

Present:
"I'm a MERN developer focused on backend — JWT auth, RBAC, and APIs that stay fast under load."

Past:
"At Techpile I built 5+ full-stack modules and fixed 20+ production bugs. Before that, a 6-month MERN internship."

Future:
"Looking for a junior role on a product team where I can own features end to end."

No childhood story. No "I've always been passionate."
A recruiter has heard50 answers that day. Be the one that's specific.

Practice it out loud. Timed. Until it sounds like you, not a resume.
