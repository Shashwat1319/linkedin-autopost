My deploy fear timeline:

Month 1: "Don't touch prod. It might explode."
Month 3: "Let me test 47 times first."
Month 6: "Where's the pipeline? Why are we clicking buttons?"

What killed the fear:
→ CI runs tests on every push — I stopped being the test
→ Staging environment = practice server
→ One-command rollback — mistakes became reversible
→ Logs visible before users report anything

Fear of deploying isn't caution. It's a missing process.

If deploying takes courage, your pipeline is too manual.

How do you deploy — manual, CI/CD, or somewhere in between?
