Uncomfortable truth about first salary (India, 2026):

Most juniors get ₹15-30k/month. Some less. It stings.

What it is NOT: a verdict on your skill.
What it IS: an entry fee — AND a negotiation you can still make.

Things that actually move the number:
→ Proof of shipping (live projects > any certificate)
→ Interview performance (DSA + system thinking)
→ Domain knowledge (fintech pays differently than CRUD shops)
→ Saying a number with confidence after "What are your expectations?"

What it doesn't move:
→ 12 more tutorial certificates
→ Waiting for the "right time"
→ Comparing yourself to someone's LinkedIn highlight reel

Learn fast, ship visibly, switch in 12-18 months with leverage.
