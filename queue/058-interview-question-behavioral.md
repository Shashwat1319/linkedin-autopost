Behavioral interviews aren't about personality. They're about evidence.

STAR isn't a template to memorize — it's proof you FINISH things:

Situation: one sentence of context
Task: what YOU specifically owned
Action: the decisions YOU made (not "we")
Result: number, or observable change

My go-to story set (prepare 5, reuse everywhere):
1. A bug that took too long — how I debugged it
2. A disagreement about code — how I resolved it
3. Something I shipped that failed — what I changed after
4. Learning a tech fast — how, with proof
5. Helping someone else succeed — teaching moment

Storyless candidates sound generic. Story-rich candidates sound experienced.

Which story from YOUR work would you tell first?
