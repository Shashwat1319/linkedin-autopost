My Friday15-minute review (unchanged for 6 months):

1. What shipped this week? (even 1 commit counts)
2. What broke, and what did I learn from it?
3. What did I avoid? (the uncomfortable one)
4. Next week's ONE priority

No fancy tool. A markdown file.

Why it works:
→ Job hunting feels like nothing happens — until you read 8 weeks of notes
→ Patterns show up: "I avoided estimation 3 weeks straight"
→ Monday starts with a decision, not a browser tab

Motivation comes from evidence. Notes are evidence.
