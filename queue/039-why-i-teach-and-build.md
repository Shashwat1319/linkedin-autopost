People ask: "You teach AND code? Why not just code?"

Because each one feeds the other.

Teaching → forces fundamentals to be honest. Can't fake loops at a whiteboard.
Building → gives me stories that actually happened to tell students.

Best classroom moment: a student found MY bug in a demo.
Best prod moment: a lesson from class fixed a race condition at work.

Two careers, one compounding loop.

Also honest: teaching pays the bills while I hunt for the dev role I want. No shame in a survival job while the dream one is in progress.
