Unfair advantage of being a developer who teaches:

I can't hand-wave.

A student asks "why React re-renders?" — and I have to KNOW.
Not "I've seen it done." KNOW — state, props, context, memoization, the WHOLE chain.

That pressure:
→ Turned my shaky areas into solid ones
→ Made me explain architecture with analogies (systems I can break down)
→ Gave me interview answers that sound like understanding, not recitation

Also: I ship LESS code than full-time devs. So I optimize for DEPTH of what I do know.

Every interview I've had — teaching stories landed hardest.
"Why should we hire a teacher?" becomes "because I can make the whole team understand anything."
