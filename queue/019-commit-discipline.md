My git history used to read like a diary of despair:

"fix"
"fix again"
"please work"
"final fix"
"final fix 2"

Then I worked on a team where commit messages had to explain the WHY.

My rules now:
→ Subject: what changed and why, under 72 chars
→ Body: context a stranger needs in 6 months
→ One logical change per commit — no "also updated readme" mixed with an auth rewrite

Six months later, git log is your project's documentation.
Future-you (and your team) will either thank or curse present-you.
