I rewrote error messages for a week. Users stopped calling.

Old:
"Error500 — something went wrong"

New:
"We couldn't save your changes. Your work is still here — hit Save again, or email support@ with this code: 4192."

Three things changed:
→ Tell them what HAPPENED (in human words)
→ Tell them what to DO next
→ Give a traceable code — support can find the log

Error messages are UI. Someone reads them at their worst moment.

Your success path gets design reviews. Your failure path gets... a console.log.

Go read your last error message. Would YOU know what to do?
