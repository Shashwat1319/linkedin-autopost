100 posts queued. The system, not the motivation:

Every night before bed, if something interesting happened at work/class/build — one note:
→ The bug and its actual cause
→ The explanation a student's question forced me to find
→ The decision I'd defend in a code review

Then once a week, notes → posts → into a queue.
A GitHub Action posts one every morning at 8:45 AM.

I don't decide daily whether to post. The decision was made ONCE.

That's the whole trick:
→ Systems remove daily negotiations with yourself
→ Motivation is a guest, systems are the house

100 posts = ~3 months of showing up without thinking about it.
