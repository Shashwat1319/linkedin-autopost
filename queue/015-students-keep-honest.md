My students keep me honest.

"Sir, this code is why?"
"Sir, if we delete this line?"
"Sir, why not just use ChatGPT?"

You cannot answer those with buzzwords.

So now before I teach anything:
→ I break it myself first
→ I know the failure mode, not just the happy path
→ I can explain it WITHOUT jargon

Teaching = forced learning. Every single day.

To every junior dev reading this: find someone to explain your work to. A friend, a junior, a camera. The gaps show up immediately.

What topic would YOU struggle to teach right now?
