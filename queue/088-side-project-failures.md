My side projects that DIED (and what each taught):

1. A notes app — killed by Notion. Lesson: don't rebuild what exists, IMPROVE a niche.
2. A social network for students — killed by zero users. Lesson: distribution IS the product.
3. An AI resume builder (early) — killed by API costs. Lesson: unit economics before features.

Survivors:
→ AutoDev — free tool, real utility, open source
→ My deal automation — solves MY pain daily

Pattern: things that survive solve a problem I have EVERY week.

Ideas are cheap. Weekly pain points are the goldmine.
