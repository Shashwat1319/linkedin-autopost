Challenge for anyone feeling stuck (it worked for me):

One week. One tiny project. Daily public progress.

Day 1: pick a REAL annoyance (yours, a friend's, your class's)
Day 2: one page/endpoint — ugly is fine
Day 3: make it do the ONE thing
Day 4: deploy it — share the link somewhere
Day 5: fix what people actually complain about
Day 6: one improvement you're proud of
Day 7: write what you learned (3 lines is enough)

Rules:
→ No new frameworks mid-week
→ No redesigning the UI daily
→ DONE beats PERFECT on every single day

Side note: "I'll build it when I have time" is where projects go to die.

Who's in? Reply with what you'd build — I'll hold you to it.
