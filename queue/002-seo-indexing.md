Got my personal site indexed on Google in under 24 hours. The playbook (works for any new site):

1. robots.txt + sitemap.xml — done before anything else
2. Submit sitemap in Google Search Console
3. Add JSON-LD structured data (Person schema for a portfolio)
4. Request indexing manually for the homepage
5. Secure headers (HTTPS, CSP) so the crawler trusts the page

Day 1: "URL is on Google."
Day 3: all 5 pages discovered from the sitemap.

SEO isn't magic — it's just removing every reason for Google to say no.
