What happens to your resume AFTER you hit submit (the ATS part):

1. Parsed into fields — skills, dates, education
2. Ranked against the job description's keywords
3. Human skim:6-10 seconds on the ones that pass

That's it. That's the gauntlet.

My resume rules after rebuilding mine:
→ Section headers machines know: Experience, Education, Skills
→ Keywords from the JOB DESCRIPTION, not a generic skills dump
→ Dates consistent format everywhere (Jan 2026 – Jun 2026, not "2026/6")
→ One page. Tables and icons break parsers
→ Real links: full URLs (portfolio, GitHub) that a bot can extract

ATS isn't the enemy. A resume written only for humans is.
