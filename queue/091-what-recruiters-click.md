Watched a recruiter screen-share (with permission). Here's the click order:

1. Your HEADLINE (2 seconds — is this the right role?)
2. Resume PDF (if headline passes —60-90 seconds)
3. Portfolio link (if resume interests them)
4. Skim EXPERIENCE dates for gaps and consistency
5. Skills/endorsements: almost never

What they did NOT open:
→ Your articles
→ Your certifications section
→ Your "About" (unless the resume confused them)

So my optimization:
→ Headline = role keywords, exactly
→ Resume = one page, ATS-clean, URL in line 1
→ Portfolio = loads in 2 seconds on mobile
→ Experience = gapless, consistent with resume

Design your profile for the60-second path, not the ideal reader.
