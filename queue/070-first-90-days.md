If I joined a team tomorrow, here's my first-week plan:

Day 1-2: RUN it locally. Break it. Fix it. That's the real onboarding.
Day 3: Trace ONE feature end to end — route → handler → DB → response.
Day 4: Read the last10 merged PRs — that's the culture, not the docs.
Day 5: Ship a tiny fix. Typo, test, log message. Get the pipeline working.

Week 2-4 goals:
→ Own one small feature with support, not supervision
→ Ask "why" out loud — juniors are ALLOWED to ask
→ Find who knows what (the human documentation)

Nobody expects production work in week one.
They expect momentum and questions that show you're tracing, not guessing.

What would YOUR first week look like?
