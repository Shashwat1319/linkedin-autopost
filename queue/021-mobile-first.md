A client once said: "Looks great on my laptop."

On his phone: buttons overlapping, tables overflowing, nav unreachable.

I had tested ONE breakpoint out of pride. He used the other one.

Now my checklist:
→ Design the smallest screen FIRST
→ 44px minimum tap targets — fingers are not cursors
→ Tables get a strategy: scroll, stack, or rewrite as cards
→ Test on an actual phone before saying "done"

60%+ of web traffic is mobile. If your site is desktop-only, you're serving half your audience an excuse.

Open YOUR site on your phone right now. What's broken?
