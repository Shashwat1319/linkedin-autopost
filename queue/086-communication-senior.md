What separates a junior who grows fast from one who stays:

It's not IQ. It's communication.

Junior pattern: disappears for 2 days, returns with wrong solution confidently.
Growth pattern: "I'm stuck on X. Tried A and B. I think C might work — 10 minutes?"

Second one gets help immediately AND learns the reasoning.

My comms rules:
→ Status updates BEFORE anyone asks
→ Blockers surfaced same day, not at deadline
→ Summaries after calls (3 bullets, who owns what)
→ Questions with options, not open-ended panic

Technical skill gets you hired.
Communication gets you promoted.

Rate yourself honestly: how long do you sit with a blocker before asking?
